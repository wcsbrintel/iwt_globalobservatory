# Prospective intelligence
The opt-in overlay appears only in expanded clean GLOBE. It does not mutate filters, cases, routes, or restricted data. It uses the live base filter (country, group, modal, search and explicit year) with a separate analytical history window. Only precise occurrence dates enter complete-month analysis. Unknown dates are reported separately. Each countable canonical case contributes one event; no quantities are combined.
## Adapters
The public registry ProspectiveIntelligence.registerSource(id, {snapshot(context)}) accepts adapters returning {rows, visible, edges}. A row contains id, canonicalId (stable cross-source deduplication key), countable (explicit boolean), day (ISO occurrence day or null), country, groups and mode. An edge contains eventId, from, to, direction and kind. Adapter code must apply the same active filter semantics before returning records and must not supply sensitive data to the public client. Keep different observation units separate: only equivalent deduplicated occurrence records may be merged. Call invalidate() when an external source refreshes. Current adapters use live canonical cases and validated documented route matches only. Future sources must supply explicit coverage, units, mapping, provenance and access rules before activation.
## Methods and limitations
Routes count distinct case IDs per country pair, with known direction respected; intended, attributed and completed movements are not proof of transit. Network centrality is distinct-country degree, not criminal-network identification. Concentration and priority are descriptive country counts, not crime risk. Group heatmap normalizes within each row. Trend compares last three complete months to previous three. Anomaly is a descriptive standardized deviation versus earlier months, not a calibrated alert. Seasonal means require 24 months and minimum sample support; uneven search coverage remains a limitation.
Forecast compares rolling 3/6-month means and same-month prior-year against naive last value using rolling-origin mean absolute error; at least 24 complete months, 60 events, 18 occupied months, 4 of last 6 occupied, 8 test origins, and performance no worse than naive are required. The 1/2/3-month output is a forecast of recorded events only, with empirical 90th-percentile absolute-error bands (not calibrated confidence intervals). Zeros represent no recorded cases, not confirmed absence. Coverage variation and retrospective ingestion may inflate apparent skill; real out-of-sample monitoring and source coverage audit are needed before operational use. No predictive local hotspots or inferred routes are published.
